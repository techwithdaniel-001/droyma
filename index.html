HOW IT WORKS

It doesn’t watch your sleep.
It works on it.

A small wearable behind your ear. Three steps, all night, at home or in a hotel bed.

1 · Senses

Senses your brain

Sleep happens in your brain, not your wrist. Soft sensors behind your ear read your brain’s deep-sleep waves (EEG).

2 · Responds

Plays a soft, timed sound

Only in deep sleep, on each wave, a very soft sound reaches you through bone conduction. Silent to the room, quiet the moment you stir.

3 · Learns

Learns your timing

Every brain is different. Night by night, Droyma gets to know yours: when to play, and how softly.

Questions

How does Droyma work, in plain words?

In deep sleep, your brain moves in slow waves. Droyma senses those waves from behind your ear. At the right moment in each one, it plays a very soft sound through bone conduction, which helps the wave run deeper. Night by night, it learns your timing. Sleep labs have studied this method, called acoustic stimulation, for over a decade.

Is it another sleep tracker?

No. Trackers tell you how you slept. Droyma is designed to change how you sleep. There’s no new score to chase. The difference is meant to show up in how you feel the next day.

I already wear an Oura or WHOOP. Why Droyma?

Your ring or strap can tell you a night went badly. It can’t change it. Droyma is built to do something about it while it’s happening: it senses your brain and responds with soft, timed sound, drug-free.

Will I hear it? Will my partner?

The sound travels through bone rather than the air. It’s designed to be barely audible to you, soft enough to sleep through, and not to fill the room.

Is it comfortable to sleep in?

Droyma is designed to sit behind your ear all night, with nothing over your head or face. Comfort testing is part of development before launch.

Is it safe?

Droyma uses very soft sound at low intensity. It’s still in development and will be safety-tested before it ships. If you’re always tired, snore heavily or stop breathing at night, talk to a doctor first. Droyma is a wellness product. It isn’t designed to diagnose or treat insomnia or any sleep disorder.

What happens to my brain data?

Your sleep signals are used to time Droyma’s sound. Before anything ships, we’ll publish exactly what’s stored, where, and how to delete it.

Is it proven?

The method is. Timed sound during sleep has been studied in sleep labs for over a decade, with the results shown above. Droyma itself is still in development, so we won’t claim results until we’ve tested it. We’ll share our own test results with the waitlist, whatever they show.

Short nights, broken nights, new time zones?

It can’t add hours or reset your body clock. It’s designed to make the hours you get count: deeper sleep, fewer wake-ups. Home, hotel or a new time zone, it goes where you go.

I’m a light sleeper. Won’t a sound wake me?

It’s designed to play only in deep sleep, when you’re hardest to wake, to stay softer than the sounds that disturb you, and to go quiet if you start to stir.

Can I stop taking melatonin or sleep supplements?

Talk to your doctor before changing anything you take. Melatonin can cause side effects for some people, like next-day drowsiness, headaches, dizziness or vivid dreams (NIH). Droyma is drug-free, but it isn’t a replacement for treatment.
