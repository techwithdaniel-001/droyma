Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect only what you give us when you join the waitlist, we use it only to build Droyma and keep you updated, and we never sell it.

Who we are

Droyma is a sleep wearable in development, built by Daniel Opata. In this policy, “Droyma”, “we” and “us” mean the Droyma team. You can reach us any time at opataebubechukwu@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • What you tell us when you join: your email address, your first name if you give it, what gets in the way of your sleep, and what you’ve tried before (for example a tracker, melatonin or a sleep mask).
  • Basic technical information: the page you signed up from. Like any website, our hosting and font providers may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads.

We don’t use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The site stores one small note in your browser so the opening animation doesn’t replay every visit.

Your sleep answers

Your answers about your sleep may count as health-related information. We collect them only with your consent, when you choose to submit the waitlist form, and use them only for the purposes below. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

How we use it

  • To send you Droyma updates: prototype progress, test results, launch timing and pricing.
  • To understand the sleep problems people want solved, so we build the right product.
  • To invite waitlist members to early access or testing, if you’d like to take part.

We never sell your information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

Who helps us

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and the waitlist. They process information only on our behalf:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • FormSubmit (and, at times, Tally) delivers waitlist sign-ups to our inbox.
  • Google serves the site’s fonts and provides our email.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until it’s no longer needed for Droyma’s launch, whichever comes first.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from any email with one click, or by emailing us.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • Withdraw your consent for us to use your sleep answers.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Washington State), you may have further rights under local privacy law. Email us and we’ll respond within 30 days. We won’t treat you differently for using your rights.

The Droyma device

Droyma will sense brain activity during sleep to time its sound. That device isn’t available yet, and this policy covers only the website and waitlist. Before anything ships, we’ll publish exactly what the device stores, where it’s kept, and how you can delete it.

Security

We use reputable providers and keep access to waitlist information limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share.

Children

The waitlist is for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above, and for meaningful changes we’ll email the waitlist.

Droyma is a wellness product in development, not a medical device.
