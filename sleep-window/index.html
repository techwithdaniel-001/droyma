  1. Go to bed only when you feel sleepy, and not before your window.

Your 6 weeks

0 of 6

One short session a week, about 10 minutes each. The diary and the window do most of the work, and the sessions explain why.

Awake and can't sleep?

Lying there trying harder teaches your brain that bed is a place for being awake. Break that link.

  1. Get up and go to another room. Keep the lights low.
  2. Do something quiet and dull: a paper book, folding laundry, a puzzle. No screens, no clock watching.
  3. Go back to bed only when your eyes feel heavy.
  4. Still awake after a while? Repeat. It's normal for this to happen a few times in the first week.

This is a test build, not medical advice. CBT-I is the first-line treatment for chronic insomnia in clinical guidelines. This prototype follows its standard parts but hasn't been clinically tested.

Don't use the sleep window without a clinician if you have bipolar disorder, seizures, untreated sleep apnea, or are pregnant. Feeling very sleepy is common in weeks 1 and 2. Don't drive or use machinery when drowsy.
